TEAM–SEASON CONTEXT

This chart always shows the full selected team and season. Down, distance, field-zone and play-type filters do not apply.

What is included in this view? ＋

Regular-season pass and run plays with a valid down, team, opponent and EPA. Quarterback scrambles, kneels and spikes are excluded. This is a filtered offensive sample, not every team snap.

EPA is expected points added, supplied by nflfastR through nflverse. Success means EPA > 0. Rates are play-weighted. The league reference uses the same selected situation and excludes the selected team. Historical results describe what happened; they do not establish what a coach should call next.

Field zones: red zone ≤ 20 yards from goal; opponent half > 20 to 50; own half > 50. Short distance includes goal-to-go at zero yards. Individual-play observations are correlated within games; no independent-play confidence interval is presented for EPA.

THE EVIDENCE

Calibration detail

Model design and limitations ＋

The constant baseline predicts the 2023–2024 training success rate for every test play. AUC measures discrimination; Brier score and log loss measure probability quality. Lower Brier/log loss and higher AUC are better. A modest AUC can coexist with a small improvement in calibration-oriented scores.

Player identity, personnel, defensive alignment, play design and injuries are absent. This is a simple benchmark, not a production scouting system. Intervals for calibration are binomial summaries; game-cluster bootstrap uncertainty for the Brier difference is conditional on the fitted model and does not include model-training uncertainty.
